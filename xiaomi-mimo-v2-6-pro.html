MiMo-V2.6-Pro: Open Weights at AA 46 — and the Terminal-Bench 4.0 Asterisk

26 September 2026 · Anpoo Sivanadi · based on Xiaomi’s Sep 21 MiMo-V2.6-Pro / Flash release and Artificial Analysis / Kingy / DataNorth reads

Xiaomi MiMo-V2.6-Pro / Flash: MIT open weights, AA Intelligence Index 46, $0.435/$0.87 list (Flash cheaper), same-day foil to Grok 4.7 — with a Terminal-Bench 4.0 gap vs Opus/Astra on Xiaomi’s own table.

This morning’s Lessengers 3.2T glass NPO note was a package next to the switch ASIC CapEx sentence. Tonight is the other half of the stack again: what you route agent traffic to once the hall is paid for — and whether that route has to stay closed. On September 21, 2026 (September 22 in Beijing / Japan coverage), Xiaomi’s MiMo team shipped MiMo-V2.6-Pro and MiMo-V2.6-Flash under an MIT license with weights on Hugging Face, the same day SpaceXAI shipped closed Grok 4.7.

Primary sources: MiMo-V2.6-Pro-RL model card, MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL, Xiaomi MiMo V2.6 page, Artificial Analysis model page, DataNorth Sep 22 read, Kingy.ai Sep 21–22 specs + AA table, Gigazine Sep 24, and Grok 4.7 launch / AA’s Grok 4.7 note for the same-day foil. Same-day foil: Grok 4.7 also scores 46 on AA at $2 / $6, 500k context, closed weights ( xAI launch ). That is the price-floor sentence Xiaomi is selling — not a claim that Pro beats Claude Opus 5.5 or GPT-6 Astra on every long-horizon board.

What actually changed for people who run agents

The durable product change is not a new FLOPS sticker. It is MIT weights + a rate card that sits under Grok’s same-day AA score.

Artificial Analysis puts Pro at 46 on Intelligence Index v4.3.x — top open-weights row in Kingy’s Sep 21 table — at about $0.13 per index task versus Grok 4.7’s ~$3.74 at xhigh and Claude Opus 5 max at ~$5.86 on that same secondary table. List tokens are $0.435 / $0.87 with a deep cache-read discount ($0.0036). Flash cuts that to $0.14 / $0.28 at 15B active. UltraSpeed is a latency SKU at 10× Pro list, not a smarter checkpoint.

Architecture explains the serving math: only 42B of 1.02T fire per token. That does not mean you can park Pro on a spare workstation — the published SGLang command is a two-node bring-up. Flash’s tp 8 card is the realistic self-host on-ramp.

MiMo does not move KV blocks and does not invent a fabric. It argues that a large slice of agent loops should not burn Astra or Opus tokens, and that the open-weights invoice finally has a named MoE sheet at the same AA Index point as Grok 4.7.

Caveats I would write into any MiMo ticket

  1. Reproduce Terminal-Bench 4.0 / DeepSWE / your repo harness before you price a SKU — Xiaomi’s table is not your fleet.
  2. Do not paste “on par with Opus 5.” Xiaomi’s own rows trail on most shared Opus 5 comparisons; the honest win is cost at AA 46, not a blanket ceiling claim. Opus 5.5 is also newer than the Opus 5 column in Xiaomi’s card.
  3. Self-host Pro ≠ free. Weights are MIT; serving still needs a multi-GPU / multi-node bill. Flash first unless you have the cluster.
  4. UltraSpeed is 10× price. Latency tool — measure tok/s on the endpoint you bill.
  5. Cyber capability is open. CyberGym 94 under MIT means strong vuln-analysis weights without an API gate — design your own controls.
  6. Not a rematch of Lessengers / PicoJool / Continuum. Those are optics and campus fabric. This is the model routing layer.

How I would evaluate if I ran agent fleets

  1. Cost per completed task on Flash and Pro vs Sol, Luna, and Opus 5.5 — not just $/MTok.
  2. Terminal-Bench 4.0-class unattended jobs where Xiaomi’s own gap is widest; keep Astra/Opus on a shadow route.
  3. Cache-hit and verbosity on your prompts — AA’s cheap $/task assumes its token mix.
  4. Self-host only after Flash proves quality on your suite; escalate to Pro weights when the cluster math clears.
  5. Guardrails in code for unattended cyber-capable open weights, not prompt warnings alone.

This is an open-weights price-floor release at Artificial Analysis 46, not a new closed flagship. Route hard unattended jobs to Astra or Opus 5.5 until your harness says otherwise. Put Flash on volume and Pro on the harder open tier when $0.13-class index economics beat the Terminal-Bench 4.0 risk. Measure, then move traffic.
